Izuki on your TV

Free for Android TV, Google TV and Fire TV.

  1. On the TV, open the app store and install the free Downloader app (by AFTVnews).
  2. Open Downloader and type nova-izuki.github.io/tv
  3. Izuki downloads — press Install (allow Downloader to install apps if the TV asks), then open Izuki from your home screen.
